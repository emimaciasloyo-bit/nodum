# Waitlist

- **Form:** Tally, https://tally.so/r/68bjpN
- **Fields:** email, and "What would you most like to stop doing by hand?"

## How to use the answers
- Count which of the four jobs people mention most. That confirms or changes the [build order](../01-Playbook/Build-Order.md).
- The first 10 sign-ups who fit get the **founding offer** (see [Pricing](Pricing.md)).
- For people who sign up, send the longer **intake form** before the discovery call (see [Client Onboarding](../01-Playbook/Client-Onboarding.md)).
- The playbook plans a `waitlist` table in Postgres. Don't confuse it with the *scheduling* waitlist used to fill cancellations.
