# Pricing

> [!WARNING]
> Not final. These are the playbook's suggestions (2026-10-09).

## Cost basis
- Hard costs: about **$10–35 per client per month**, plus 1–2 hours a month of monitoring. Breakdown in [Stack & Costs](../01-Playbook/Stack-and-Costs.md).
- Before client 1: about **$15/month** (server $6, domain about $1, Claude testing about $5, Twilio test number about $1–2).

**Price on value, not cost.** A part-time assistant costs $1,500+ a month, one recovered invoice is often $500+, and one caught lead for a trade is often $1,000+.

## Suggested plans
| Plan | Includes | Setup | Monthly | Margin after hard costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1 automation + Friday summary | $500 | $199 | ~90% |
| Growth | any 2–3 automations | $900 | $399 | ~92% |
| Full desk | all 4 | $1,200 | $549 | ~94% |

## Founding offer (waitlist)
- Setup **50% off**, and **30% off monthly for 12 months**.
- In return: a testimonial, a case study with real numbers, and a 3-month minimum.
- Capped at the **first 10 clients**.

## Other rules
- Texts included up to 500 segments a month; above that, cost + 20%.
- Adding an automation later: $300 setup, +$150/month.
- Don't promise outcomes ("we'll collect $X"). Promise the service ("every overdue invoice gets 3 reminders with a pay link").
- Bill monthly in advance by card or ACH on a Stripe subscription.
