# The Promises We Make

What the landing page promises, and so what we must be able to deliver.

## 1. Invoice chasing
- Reminders on **day 1, 7 and 14** after the due date, written in the owner's tone.
- **Payment link included every time.**
- Customers who still don't pay are flagged to the owner.
- **Monday "who owes what" summary.**
- Accounting: QuickBooks, Xero, Stripe.

How: [Invoice Chasing](../01-Playbook/Invoice-Chasing.md)

## 2. Lead follow-up
- Reply to new enquiries (website forms, email, missed calls) within **about 5 minutes**.
- Ask for budget, timing and address up front.
- Book a call onto the calendar.
- **Follow up twice** if they go quiet.

How: [Lead Follow-Up](../01-Playbook/Lead-Follow-Up.md)

## 3. Inbox triage
- Sort emails into orders, questions, invoices and spam.
- Auto-answer routine questions in the owner's voice.
- Send anything unusual or sensitive (refunds, complaints) to the owner with a suggested reply. **Never sends anything sensitive on its own.**

How: [Inbox Triage](../01-Playbook/Inbox-Triage.md)

## 4. Scheduling
- Bookings from email, text or the website.
- Reminders the day before every appointment.
- Clients can reschedule by replying.
- Fill cancellations from a waitlist.

How: [Scheduling](../01-Playbook/Scheduling.md)

## Cross-cutting promises
- **Works with:** Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, QuickBooks, Xero, Stripe, Shopify.
- **Review mode first:** every task starts with the owner approving drafts, and can be switched to **autopilot** per task (and back). See [Review Mode & Autopilot](../01-Playbook/Review-Mode-and-Autopilot.md).
- **Setup in about 3 weeks**, with about an hour of the owner's time in total:
  - Week 1: 30-minute discovery call
  - Week 2: build and connect
  - Week 3: review mode
  - After: autopilot plus a **Friday summary email**
- **Customer data:** we only connect the accounts a task needs, with permission, and the owner can disconnect at any time.
- **Pricing:** not final; waitlist members get founding pricing locked in and see every detail before anything is charged.
